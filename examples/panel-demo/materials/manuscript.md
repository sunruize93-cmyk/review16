# Synthetic manuscript fixture: A tiny routing rule

**FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION.** This text and every accompanying review are invented
to exercise the Review16 interface. No research, experiment, model execution,
human review, acceptance outcome or measured performance is represented here.

## 1. Motivation

Consider a toy router that chooses between two branches. This fixture asks whether
an explicit rule and a bounded explanation can make that choice inspectable.

## 2. A toy argument

Under the stated assumption that every input belongs to exactly one of two sets,
the router selects exactly one branch. This follows directly from the partition
assumption. It is a local illustration, not a new theorem or research contribution.

## 3. Illustration and missing evidence

Two handcrafted inputs illustrate the rule. There is no dataset, real benchmark,
statistical estimate, claim of computational benefit, or deployment result.
Behavior under nondeterministic transitions is intentionally unspecified.

## 4. Scope

The example establishes only what follows from the toy partition assumption.
It cannot establish usefulness, empirical reliability or cross-context transfer.

The numeric values in the accompanying review JSON files are fixed UI fixtures.
They must not be interpreted as a real assessment of this text or of Review16.
