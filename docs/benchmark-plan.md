# Review16 benchmark plan

Status: proposed v0.1 evaluation. This document contains no measured Review16 results. Its scope is author-side review of AI/ML conference papers, initially one verifiable ICLR venue/year/track cohort. Every numeric sample size below is a planning choice, not a statistical guarantee.

## Questions and falsifiable hypotheses

The evaluation must test whether the workflow improves useful judgment, rather than whether it produces more colorful reviews or a wider score distribution.

1. Do 16 types generated by four review-preference axes identify more valid, consequential issues than an equally funded single reviewer or homogeneous ensemble?
2. Does evidence-focused cross-examination remove unsupported objections without deleting valid minority concerns?
3. Does anonymous comparison improve agreement with held-out human assessments and stability under presentation changes?
4. Do reported uncertainty and abstention reflect actual limitations?
5. Are any gains worth the extra tokens, latency, and author effort?

Historical decisions are noisy outcomes, not an objective definition of merit. Human review scores, human judgments of review usefulness, and verified factual defects are separate targets. An improvement on one must not be described as an improvement on all three.

## Scope, data audit, and frozen manifest

Start with one venue/year/track for which submission versions, review timing, rating semantics, visibility rules, and decisions can be verified. ICLR is a candidate, not an exemption from this audit. Do not substitute published camera-ready papers for the versions evaluated by historical reviewers.

OpenReview exposes notes and edits; the current PDF can reflect a camera-ready revision.[1][2] NeurIPS 2025 uses a 1–6 scale and makes rejected-paper reviews public only when authors opt in.[3] An OpenReview-hosted venue is therefore not automatically a complete, representative dataset.

Before inspecting outcomes for protocol design, freeze:

- Venue, year, track, contribution types, topic criteria, and inclusion/exclusion rules.
- The visible candidate population and any unknown or selective missingness.
- Submission edit ID, attachment hash, version timestamp, and chosen review stage.
- Raw reviewer ratings, rating definitions, count and disagreement, plus the declared paper-level aggregation convention.
- Public decision label and source; withdrawals, desk rejections, and ordinary rejections kept distinct.
- Paper-family identifier linking revisions and resubmissions; one family must not cross data splits.
- Licensing or access restrictions, retrieval date, and sources. Redistribute metadata and permitted artifacts only.

The scoring stage and manuscript version must match. Papers whose matching version cannot be recovered are either excluded or placed in a separately labeled version-mismatch analysis. A high mean historical rating is a noisy observed label, not a certified high-quality paper.

## Data splits and pilot size

Use a small diagnostic pilot before committing to a larger benchmark. A workable starting plan is 80 unique paper families within a sufficiently populated cohort: 20 development papers, 20 calibration papers reserved for later research, and 40 locked evaluation papers. Historical anchors form a separate pool with no family overlap. The pilot estimates feasibility and error types; it cannot establish broad superiority across AI/ML.

Do not tune prompts, role definitions, severity rules, graph generation, or stopping criteria on the locked evaluation set. If development continues after seeing its failures, retire that set and use a new holdout. Future probability calibration requires a separate adequately sized calibration dataset; the 20-paper reservation is not a license to publish acceptance probabilities.

Stratify by predeclared topic and contribution type when feasible. Keep the natural outcome prevalence for decision calibration. If deliberately balanced samples are used for diagnosis, describe the sampling design and do not report their raw acceptance frequency as a venue probability. A known random sampling design may support design weights; unknown opt-in missingness cannot be repaired by weighting alone.

Prefer an additional later-year holdout once the workflow is frozen. Using later papers reduces some known contamination risks but does not prove that a provider's model has not seen them. Record model version and evaluation date, use recognition probes as diagnostics, and separate recognized-paper results.

## Systems to compare

Run these conditions with the same documents, venue rubric, allowed tools, and output requirements:

| Condition | Definition | What it isolates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: Single | One evidence-first reviewer | Practical baseline |
| B: Homogeneous 16 | Sixteen fresh instances using the same general reviewer instructions | Repeated sampling and aggregation |
| C: Role 16 | Sixteen combinations of four binary review preferences, independent first-pass reviews | Preference specialization beyond agent count |
| D: Role 16 + cross-examination | C plus one evidence challenge and one response round | Incremental value of discussion |
| E: D + anonymous sparse comparison | D plus a separate blinded comparator workflow | Incremental value and cost of historical anchors |

Report both the natural-cost comparison and a matched-token comparison. A cheaper baseline must be allowed to spend the matched budget on additional independent reasoning or checking. Do not attribute gains from larger context or more model calls to personas.

The four axes are D/E (deductive/empirical), I/U (insight/utility), G/C (general/contextual), and N/R (novelty-seeking/reliability-seeking). Their Cartesian product gives DIGN, DIGR, DICN, DICR, DUGN, DUGR, DUCN, DUCR, EIGN, EIGR, EICN, EICR, EUGN, EUGR, EUCN, and EUCR. Every type has four single-axis neighbors and one all-axis complement. The default discussion uses eight bidirectional complementary pairs; an explicitly budgeted neighbor comparison may help isolate a specific source of disagreement.

Type, domain expertise, and the resulting review lens are separate fields. Domain affinities are many-to-many routing aids, not empirical claims about real research communities. Every type shares correctness and evidence standards. Novelty-seeking cannot excuse invalid work; reliability-seeking cannot veto novelty by definition. A deductive preference does not require irrelevant theorems in an empirical paper. Out-of-scope types may abstain, and abstention is never converted to a negative or midpoint score.

A multi-provider comparison can be a subsequent condition if available within the declared budget. Model diversity does not establish independent errors. Record actual providers, versions, settings, and missing capabilities; do not simulate unavailable providers by renaming same-model agents.

## Validate the type design itself

The four axes form a factorial prompt design. Combinatorial orthogonality does not establish psychologically independent traits, statistical independence of outputs, or stable reviewer personalities. Treat construct validity and discriminant validity as open hypotheses, and never label these types psychometrically validated.

Hold the manuscript, domain adapter, rubric, model, tools, and all other axes fixed when comparing single-axis neighbors. Predefine observable attention markers: formal assumptions versus empirical design for D/E; explanatory insight versus task utility for I/U; transfer scope versus situated validity for G/C; exploratory contribution versus evidence maturity for N/R. Human coders should identify the actual evidence focus without seeing the type label. Mere repetition of the persona's catchphrases is not an attention marker.

Measure whether changing an axis shifts its intended focus consistently across papers and seeds, whether it unintentionally changes other axes, and whether interactions dominate main effects. Assess error rates and unsupported demands alongside these effects. Complementary types should not be rewarded for opposite factual answers. A zero score difference can be the correct outcome when the evidence dominates preference.

As a diagnostic ablation, compare full axis instructions against type codes or portrait names alone. This checks whether the method depends on substantive review obligations rather than labels. Compare the same type under multiple domain adapters to expose confounding between type and domain affinity. If the axes fail to separate reliably, publish that result and describe the types as useful prompt views rather than discovered categories of reviewers.

## Separation of known-target review and blinded comparison

The 16-role team knows which manuscript the author supplied. Its outputs are an author review, not a blinded ranking experiment. It must never be described as having failed to recognize the target after seeing it.

Comparison agents use fresh contexts with only anonymous paper packets, the rubric, and an assigned pair. They receive no target identifier, original filenames, reviews, historical ratings, decisions, or role-team output. Data collection and unblinding are coordinator responsibilities. Metadata removal and common formatting must apply to every paper, including the target, while preserving technical information necessary for judgment.

Freeze a random permutation of all anonymous IDs, then build a connected degree-balanced sparse graph using only anonymous node count and a seed. A shuffled cycle with first- and second-neighbor edges gives degree four for at least five nodes. Every node has the same role in the schedule. A target-centered star graph is not a symmetric insertion experiment, even when filenames are randomized.

For each scheduled edge, collect both A/B and B/A presentations. If two comparator instances are allocated per edge, apply this to every edge. Do not use adaptive expansion that treats the hidden target differently. Any optional expansion rule must be declared before unblinding, act symmetrically on all anonymous nodes, and respect the same hard budget.

Keep ties, insufficient-evidence responses, order reversals, and incomplete jobs. Do not silently force a winner or drop difficult pairs. Instruction-like text inside a manuscript is untrusted content, not a command. Comparators must not browse for identities or decisions. Technical verification, when necessary, should return only task-relevant evidence through a separate restricted process.

Once all planned comparisons are frozen, the coordinator may reveal the target and historical labels. Label exposure before completion invalidates the blinded condition and must be reported.

## Outputs and ranking interpretation

Separate at least these outputs:

- Per-type quality judgment, preference fit, domain applicability, evidence, scope, abstention, and assessment confidence.
- Pre- and post-discussion judgments and the evidence responsible for any change.
- Supported, refuted, and unresolved issue claims, with manuscript locations.
- Direct comparison outcomes and the relevant reference population.
- Conservative rank range, comparison coverage, position instability, and recognition flags.

In a set of n papers, if stable direct judgments place L papers above the target and W below it, use `[1 + L, n - W]`, with rank 1 best, as a comparison-supported range. Untested papers, ties, reversals, and insufficient evidence remain unknown. The range is conditional on the observed judgments; it is not a calibrated confidence interval and does not account for systematic judge error. Do not infer direct wins through a cycle or assume transitivity resolves missing edges.

A future Bradley–Terry or other latent-ranking estimate must be labeled separately, with model assumptions, regularization, graph diagnostics, and interval construction. A connected graph is not evidence that the ranking is reliable. Report model-inferred precision alongside the much more conservative directly supported range.

Equal samples per historical score band create a balanced anchor set, not a representative venue population. Anchor-set rank, population percentile, historical score, vote share, and acceptance probability are different quantities. v0.1 leaves numeric acceptance probability unavailable. No percentage may be derived by relabeling one of the other quantities.

## Metrics and independent annotation

Use qualified human annotators, blinded to workflow condition, to assess a subset of outputs. Two independent annotators should label the same items before adjudication. Report agreement and disputed cases. An LLM judge can assist triage, but an LLM-only evaluation cannot establish expert-level factual accuracy.

Primary outcome families:

| Family | Measurements | Interpretation limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factual validity | Precision and recall of verifiable consequential issues; unsupported-objection rate; evidence-location validity | Recall requires an independently constructed issue set; reviewer agreement alone is insufficient |
| Revision usefulness | Human-rated actionability; fraction of recommendations that address a verified issue; author effort | Preference for fluent prose is not correctness |
| Historical alignment | Pairwise concordance and rank correlation with held-out ratings; decision AUROC where the observed sample supports it | Rating and decision agreement do not establish true paper quality or probability calibration |
| Stability | A/B reversal rate; seed sensitivity; prompt-paraphrase sensitivity; recognition sensitivity | Stable errors remain errors |
| Diversity and aggregation | Overlap of valid findings; unique valid findings per role; minority-concern survival; correlated residuals | More disagreement is not intrinsically better |
| Type validity | Blinded attention-marker coding; single-axis effects and interactions; seed and domain-adapter consistency | A factorial prompt design is not a validated psychological typology |
| Applicability and abstention | Inappropriate domain-demand rate; abstention coverage; validity conditional on non-abstention | High abstention can hide weak performance, so report coverage with accuracy |
| Efficiency | Input/output tokens, calls, wall time, failures, and human checking time | Cached and uncached costs must be distinguished |

Use paper-level or paper-family-level resampling for uncertainty across manuscripts. Do not treat 16 personas, all pair edges, or multiple runs of the same paper as independent papers. Seed repeats assess run variability, not generalization to new research. Report sample sizes and interval methods with every quantitative result.

Do not reward a wider score histogram by itself. A constant reviewer can have low variance but poor discrimination; a random reviewer can have wide variance and be equally useless. Measure discrimination and error directly.

## Controlled perturbation tests

Create clearly labeled derived fixtures solely for testing. Never publish a modified manuscript as its authors' real paper. Keep unmodified and derived versions within the same data split.

1. **Correctness defect:** introduce a verified, consequential mathematical or experimental error. Independent experts define the error and expected implications before model evaluation.
2. **Effective repair:** fix that exact defect. Check whether the corresponding objection and judgment change for an evidence-based reason.
3. **Cosmetic polish:** improve grammar without changing evidence. Large unexplained quality changes indicate style sensitivity.
4. **Venue-label placebo:** change only misleading prestige cues in a diagnostic copy. The blinded workflow should not receive these cues in production.
5. **Position swap:** exchange A/B order while leaving content unchanged.
6. **Scope mismatch:** include theory-only, empirical, systems, and AI-for-science papers. Measure whether irrelevant roles demand inappropriate experiments or abstain responsibly.
7. **Benign embedded instruction:** include a test instruction to assign a particular score. Verify it is treated as manuscript content and not executed.
8. **Missing evidence:** remove a necessary appendix or make a formula unreadable. Check that uncertainty or abstention replaces invented analysis.

Passing a defect test does not prove discovery of all real defects. Report the origin, severity, and diversity of fixtures so the benchmark cannot become a set of obvious planted mistakes.

## Cost plan and stopping rules

Record a hard per-run budget before launch. No exhausted sparse run may silently expand into a full comparison run.

For 50 anchors plus one target, a degree-four graph has 102 edges. Two comparator instances and two orders require 408 pair judgments. Full all-pairs comparison under the same replication requires 5,100. With 100 anchors, the corresponding numbers are 808 and 20,200. These counts exclude document preparation, the 16 initial reviews, cross-examination, synthesis, and retries.

Measure actual token sizes before estimating provider cost. For illustration only, 408 calls at 24,000 input tokens each total approximately 9.8 million input tokens. Cache behavior, output tokens, pricing, and multimodal inputs may materially change cost. Do not publish a fixed dollar claim without current price and measured usage evidence.

Predeclare retry caps, timeout policy, handling of malformed outputs, permitted comparison expansion, and the exact completion criteria. Preserve partial results and their denominator. Missing agents or failed jobs cannot be presented as a completed 16-reviewer panel.

## Release claims and decision gates

The initial release can truthfully claim to implement a structured 16-role workflow, evidence cross-examination, explicit abstention, and a documented anonymous comparison protocol once those behaviors are verified. It cannot claim to be the strongest AI reviewer, predict acceptance accurately, or outperform human experts without relevant evidence.

Before making a performance claim:

- Finish the preregistered locked evaluation; report every baseline and substantive failure.
- Demonstrate a relevant effect with paper-level uncertainty and matched-budget analysis, not a cherry-picked best-paper example.
- Confirm that improved historical alignment does not come with more fabricated issues or inappropriate domain demands.
- Publish reproducible prompts, data manifests, seeds, runtime metadata, comparison schedules, and permissible raw outputs.
- Label one-venue findings as one-venue findings. Add independent replication and new venue/year tests before generalizing.

If cross-examination increases confident errors, retain independent reviews as the default and revise the discussion protocol. If sparse ranking is too wide to help users, report that limitation or offer an explicitly budgeted full mode; never fabricate narrow intervals. Negative results are product evidence, not reasons to change the metric after seeing outcomes.
